# Privacy Policy — German Vocab

**Last updated: August 3, 2026**

German Vocab ("the App") is developed by Vishesh Srivastava ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App handles.

## Summary

German Vocab does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The App works entirely offline.

## Data we do NOT collect

- No account or sign-in is required or offered.
- No personal information (name, email, location, contacts, etc.) is requested or collected.
- No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included.
- No data is sent to us or to any third party.

## Data stored on your device

The App saves your **flashcard review progress** (which words you've marked as reviewed) locally on your device, using standard Android app storage. This data:

- Never leaves your device.
- Is not accessible to us or anyone else.
- Is deleted automatically if you uninstall the App, or manually at any time using the in-app "Reset Progress" button.

## Text-to-speech (pronunciation)

The App uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine to pronounce German words aloud. This runs entirely on-device; no audio or text is sent to any server.

## Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data from anyone at all.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if a future version of the App adds a feature that uses data differently), we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
